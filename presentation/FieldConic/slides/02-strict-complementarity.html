Region I: Ball Near a Strictly Complementary Solution (SDP)

Theorem ()

Given an SDP with \(\theta=(A,b,C)\), fix \(\sigma>0\) for ADMM. Let \((\bar X_{\mathrm{SC}},\bar y_{\mathrm{SC}},\bar S_{\mathrm{SC}})\) be a strictly complementary (SC) KKT point and set \(\bar Z_{\mathrm{SC}}=\bar X_{\mathrm{SC}}-\sigma\bar S_{\mathrm{SC}}\) (a nonsingular state).

For all sufficiently small \(r>0\), \(\kappa_\theta(\operatorname{Ball}_r(\bar Z_{\mathrm{SC}}))>0\), where \(\operatorname{Ball}_r(\bar Z_{\mathrm{SC}}):=\{Z\in\Sn^n:\norm{Z-\bar Z_{\mathrm{SC}}}_F\leq r\}\).

Consequently, ADMM converges locally R-linearly after entering this forward-invariant ball.

  • First guarantee under SC alone: no nondegeneracy; nonunique optima allowed.
  • A posteriori check: test nonsingularity of the computed limiting state, \(\lambda_{\min}(|\bar Z|):=\min_i|\lambda_i(\bar Z)|>0\).
