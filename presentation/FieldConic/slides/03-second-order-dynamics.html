Region II: Ball Near a Singular Solution

Proposition ()

There exists an SDP with data \(\theta=(A,b,C)\) such that \(\operatorname{KKT}_{\mathrm{SC}}(\theta)\ne\varnothing\), and for any fixed ADMM penalty \(\sigma>0\), its fixed-point set \(\ZZ_\theta\) contains a singular optimal state \(\bar Z\) satisfying

\[\kappa_\theta\bigl(\operatorname{Ball}_r(\bar Z)\bigr)=0\qquad\text{for every }r>0.\]

Vanishing Sharpness in the Difference of Two Convex Cones

Assume \(\operatorname{KKT}_{\mathrm{SC}}(\theta)\ne\varnothing\) and fix \(\bar Z\in\ZZ_\theta\). The first-order directional derivative is

\[\delta_\theta'(\bar Z;H):=\lim_{t\downarrow0}\frac{\delta_\theta(\bar Z+tH)-\delta_\theta(\bar Z)}{t}.\]

Define two closed convex cones:

\[\begin{aligned}\mathcal T(\bar Z)&:=\overline{\{\lambda(Z-\bar Z):Z\in\ZZ_\theta,\ \lambda\geq0\}}&&\text{(tangent directions)},\\\mathcal C(\bar Z)&:=\{H\in\Sn^n:\delta_\theta'(\bar Z;H)=0\}&&\text{(stalled first-order directions)}.\end{aligned}\]

Always \(\mathcal T(\bar Z)\subseteq\mathcal C(\bar Z)\). Assume they are unequal and choose \(H\in\mathcal C(\bar Z)\setminus\mathcal T(\bar Z)\).

  • \(H\in\mathcal C(\bar Z)\) means \(\delta_\theta'(\bar Z;H)=0\).
  • \(H\notin\mathcal T(\bar Z)\), so \(d_H:=\dist(H,\mathcal T(\bar Z))>0\).

Consider sharpness modulus along the ray \(\bar Z+tH\) as \(t\downarrow0\):

\[\frac{\norm{\delta_\theta(\bar Z+tH)}_F}{\dist(\bar Z+tH,\ZZ_\theta)}=\frac{o(t)}{t\,d_H+o(t)}\longrightarrow0.\]
  • LP: \(\mathcal C(\bar z)=\mathcal T(\bar z)\) at every fixed point.
  • SDP: the cones are equal if (a) \(\ZZ_\theta\) is a singleton, or (b) \(\bar Z\) is SC.

Are there SDP instances where the two cones are not equal?
