Field Rigidity Characterizes Setwise Sharpness

Theorem (Two-Sided Field Rigidity )

Assume the primal and dual optimal sets are nonempty and compact, and an SC optimal pair \((X_{\mathrm{SC}},S_{\mathrm{SC}})\) satisfies \(0<\rank X_{\mathrm{SC}}<n\). Both optima may be nonunique. Fix \(\sigma>0\), let \(L=\spanop(\ZZ_\theta-\ZZ_\theta)\), and define

\[\mu_{\ri}:=\inf_{\bar Z\in\ri\ZZ_\theta,\ H\in L^\perp,\ \norm H_F=1}\norm{\delta_\theta'(\bar Z;H)}_F.\]
\[ \mu_{\ri}>0 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \kappa_\theta(\ZZ_\theta+\operatorname{Ball}_r(0))>0\quad\text{for some }r>0 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \mathcal C(\bar Z)=\mathcal T(\bar Z)\quad\text{for every }\bar Z\in\ZZ_\theta. \]
  • Intuition: A fixed SC pair controls both primal and dual directions; exact PSD projection secants extend interior sharpness to singular boundary points.
  • verifies rigidity for uniquely rank-one exact Boolean Moment–SOS relaxations in quotient form with one SC optimal SOS certificate (\(\rightarrow\) eventual linear convergence, even at singular limits).
  • Conjecture: Rigidity holds for a broad class of Moment–SOS relaxations of polynomial optimization problems.
