A Two-Variable LP (Continued)

\[\min_{x\geq0}x_1\quad\text{s.t. }x_1-x_2=\blue{\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}},\qquad\blue{\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}}\geq0.\]

For ADMM with \(\sigma=1\), fixed points encode KKT solutions as \(z=x-s\).

At \(\varepsilon=0\)

\[x^\star=(0,0),\qquad y^\star\in[0,1].\]

ADMM’s fixed set is a segment:

\[\ZZ_0=L=\{(y-1,-y):0\leq y\leq1\}.\]

At any \(\varepsilon>0\)

\[x^\star=(\varepsilon,0),\qquad y^\star=1.\]

Only one fixed point survives:

\[\ZZ_\varepsilon=\{(\varepsilon,-1)\}.\]

A tiny data change collapses a segment of fixed points to one point.

Slow Region Families: Persistence Away from Optimality

Proposition (Persistence Away from the Solution Set )

Assume \(T_\varepsilon\) is averaged in \(Q_\varepsilon\), with uniform bounds \(m\Id\preceq Q_\varepsilon\preceq M\Id\) (\(0<m\leq M<\infty\)).

\[\text{For any }\beta\in(0,1),\qquad N_\varepsilon:=\left\lfloor(1-\beta)\sqrt{m/M}\,\rho_\varepsilon^{-1}\right\rfloor\longrightarrow\infty.\]

Every orbit \(z^{k+1}=T_\varepsilon(z^k)\) starting at \(z^0\in\region_\varepsilon\) satisfies

\[\blue{\dist(z^k,\ZZ_\varepsilon)\geq\beta\sqrt{m/M}\,\dist(z^0,\ZZ_\varepsilon)},\qquad0\leq k\leq N_\varepsilon.\]

The orbit may leave \(\region_\varepsilon\); the distance bound persists. For the ADMM map used here, \(m=M=1\).

When field motion is small relative to the separation of optimal sets, the petal leaves a slow afterimage.

Three Geometric Cases of Afterimage

1. Face Selection

Freeze the petal. The center’s solutions collapse onto a face of the petal’s solution set.

Trimmed parts of the old solution set remain slow.

2. Hölder Sensitivity

Freeze the center. Petal solutions move much farther than the data change.

The center’s field is weak at those displaced solutions.

3. Escape to Infinity

Center solutions recede to infinity. The petal may have no solution.

Drift is small relative to the distant center solutions.
